# The AI Regulation Paradox

<p class="slide-subtitle">The EU AI Act tried to protect open source innovation. It accidentally created a loophole.</p>

---

# The good intention

<span class="pill-tag">EU AI Act</span>

Open source AI models are **exempt** from transparency requirements.

Unless they pose systemic risk.

The goal: let researchers and small companies innovate freely.

---

# What actually happened

Big Tech exploits this exemption to **dodge compliance costs**.

The "open source" label becomes a legal shield — not a transparency commitment.

---

# Who pays the price

The original developers are off the hook.

Responsibility shifts to **downstream users**.

<div class="highlight-box">

Small and medium businesses get crushed by the compliance burden they never created.

</div>

---

# The transfer mechanism

> "Obtaining open source status offers a way to escape the most burdensome requirements for technical documentation."

Big Tech gets the exemption. SMEs get the liability.

---

# The uncomfortable truth

<hr class="gold-rule" />

Protecting innovation cannot mean creating **opacity zones**.

"Open weights" without data transparency is not openness.

It's **regulatory arbitrage**.
